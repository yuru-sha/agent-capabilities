# Display Ads (Guaranteed) trafficking

Display Ads (Guaranteed) is not just an auction campaign with different bidding. It is a reservation product where placement, period, and delivery volume are secured in advance.

Examples on the current product page include LINE Talk Head View, LINE NEWS Top Ad, Yahoo! JAPAN top-page Brand Panel, and Topics PR.

## Plan before trafficking

Resolve:
- exact product / placement;
- sales period and applicable media sheet;
- guaranteed delivery unit, volume, and campaign dates;
- advertiser / agency / billing ownership;
- creative format and submission deadline;
- landing destination and measurement requirements;
- review lead time and replacement rules;
- order/booking identifier and responsible operator;
- report delivery method and expected metrics.

Reservation inventory and specifications can be time-bounded. Always use the media sheet and submission rules for the exact sales period.

## Operational boundary

The reservation workflow can include order creation, booking, trafficking, report delivery, and billing systems/processes outside the normal auction UI. Do not invent an automated booking or activation step when current official documentation requires an agency portal, sales contact, or a dedicated process.
